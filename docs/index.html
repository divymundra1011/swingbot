Calls for the next session

Strategy: sharp 3-day drop (8-25%), volume above 2× average, delivery above 40%. The paper book buys at the next open if it is inside the entry zone. Size is set by a 1.5% account risk per trade, a 6% open-risk cap and per-stock caps.

Exit rules: stop loss at 3× ATR below entry, target at 3× the risk, or sell at the close on day 15, whichever comes first. “Take” is the system’s sizing verdict given your cash and open risk.

Backtest: why this is paper-only
